# The Garden Door 🌿🚪

An interactive narrative garden experience where each click or hover interaction with plants triggers visual and auditory feedback. Tend your garden, watch it transform, and discover doors to memories.

## 🌐 Live Demo

**Play now:** [https://manasvinoronha99.github.io/The-garden-door/](https://manasvinoronha99.github.io/The-garden-door/)

![The Garden Door Concept](./the%20garden%20door%20lo-fi.png)

## 🌱 Concept

You are a gardener tending to a surreal garden where each plant you nurture grows into a doorway leading to a memory or alternate life. Every action changes the garden's mood and color, creating a unique emotional landscape that reflects your choices.

## ✨ Features

### Interactive Elements
- **6 Unique Plants**: Each with its own name, memory, and characteristics
  - Fern of Childhood
  - Vine of Lost Chances
  - Bloom of First Love
  - Thistle of Regret
  - Moss of Quiet Moments
  - Rose of Transformation

### Player Actions
1. **💧 Water** - Nurture the plant, help it grow
2. **✂️ Prune** - Refine and shape the plant
3. **🚪 Enter Door** - Step through to relive a memory (appears after watering + pruning)
4. **🍂 Neglect** - Let the plant fade and desaturate

### Dynamic Feedback
- **Visual Affordances**
  - Custom cursor changes when hovering over plants
  - Glowing outlines signal interactive elements
  - Animated vine growth
  - Blooming flowers with unique colors
  - Gradual desaturation for neglected plants

- **Auditory Feedback**
  - Ambient background tones
  - Sound cues for each interaction
  - Soothing procedurally generated audio using Web Audio API

- **Time Progression**
  - Garden cycles through times of day: Dawn → Day → Dusk → Night
  - Each interaction advances time
  - Lighting and atmosphere change subtly

### Garden States

Your final garden reflects your emotional journey:

#### 🌸 Lush Garden of Remembrance
Achieved by nurturing plants more than neglecting them. A vibrant landscape of remembered moments.

#### 🍂 Garden of Letting Go
Achieved by neglecting more plants. A muted, sparse space - the peace of forgetting.

#### 🌀 Garden of Fragments
Achieved by entering too many doors. A beautiful chaos of overlapping timelines.

#### ⚖️ Balanced Garden
Achieved by maintaining balance. Natural rhythm of growth and decay.

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Open `index.html`** in a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
2. **Click "Enter the Garden"** to begin
3. **Click on a plant** to select it
4. **Choose an action** from the interaction panel at the bottom
5. **Watch your garden transform** based on your choices
6. **Explore memories** by entering doors that appear
7. **Discover your outcome** when the journey is complete

## 🎮 Gameplay Tips

- **Experiment**: Try different combinations of actions
- **Observe**: Watch how the garden's mood changes with each interaction
- **Listen**: Audio cues provide feedback for your actions
- **Explore**: Don't be afraid to enter doors and discover memories
- **Reflect**: Your final garden tells a story about your choices

## 🌟 Key Interactions

1. **Hover over plants** - See their names and get visual feedback
2. **Water + Prune a plant** - A magical door appears
3. **Enter a door** - Experience a memory and advance the narrative
4. **Neglect a plant** - Watch it fade and contribute to a barren outcome
5. **Nurture multiple plants** - Create a lush, vibrant garden

## 📊 Stats Tracked

- **Nurtured**: Plants that grew doors (watered + pruned)
- **Neglected**: Plants you chose to let fade
- **Doors Entered**: Memories you chose to relive
